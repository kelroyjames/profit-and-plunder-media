## Long video (Wed 25 Nov 17:00)

Title: Binance: The $4.3 Billion Compliance Gap and the Checks That Help

Description:
In November 2023, Binance, then the world's largest crypto exchange, pleaded guilty in the United States and agreed to pay more than $4bn. The Justice Department said users could for years open an account with only an email address, and that Binance never filed a suspicious activity report with FinCEN. Treasury found it failed to report well over 100,000 suspicious transactions and executed more than 1.67 million trades between US persons and users in sanctioned jurisdictions or blocked persons.

This case study sets out what the US authorities published, and three checks for anyone who relies on a platform, exchange or payment provider for part of their compliance.
