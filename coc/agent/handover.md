# Chain of Custody — publishing handover

You are picking up a running content operation for **Kelroy James**, a supply chain assurance professional in the UK. He publishes under the brand **Chain of Custody**, a free weekly newsletter on supply chain controls. This document tells you how the operation works, what is live, and the rules you must follow.

## 1. Non-negotiable rules

1. **Primary sources only.** Every factual claim must come from the primary source report (e.g. the Serious Fraud Office release, NAO report, parliamentary committee report, company press release, GOV.UK). Check each claim against the source before anything is published. Never add, round up, or embellish a fact the source does not state. If a claim cannot be found in a primary source, cut it.
2. **Interpretation is labelled as such.** Lessons and checks are framed as advice, never as facts about the case.
3. **OPSEC.** Kelroy serves in the Royal Navy. No Defence, Royal Navy, unit, platform, role or operational detail in any published material. Personal experience may shape a topic, but only published sources supply the evidence.
4. **Voice.** UK English (-ise, whilst, programme, per cent). Long, measured sentences with connectives (However, Consequently). Avoid AI tics: "not X but Y", "what stays with me", "worth sitting with", "the interesting part is", tidy three-part rhetorical lists, punchy two-beat lines. Hedge only genuinely uncertain claims.
5. **LinkedIn:** native posts only (never LinkedIn Articles). Personal opening lines are welcome when Kelroy approves them. **No disclaimer and no "uses AI" line in LinkedIn posts.**
6. **Approval.** Do not publish, delete or schedule anything without Kelroy's explicit go-ahead for that item.
7. **Free tools only.** Do not buy plans or upgrades.

## 3. Production workflow

1. **Pick a case** with a clear primary source. Fetch and read the source in full.
2. **Fact sheet first.** List every number, date, name and quote with the exact source wording. Anything not in the source is out.
3. **Script** (~420–450 words, ~2:45 of narration): opening (what happened, dated), Part I–III/IV sections, three practical checks, slogan line, call to action ("Every week, Chain of Custody breaks down one supply chain control like this. It's free, and the link is in the description.").
4. **Re-check the script line by line** against the fact sheet.
5. **Narration:** Clipchamp text-to-speech, voice *Christopher Multilingual*, English (United Kingdom), pace slider 9; export audio only.
6. **Visuals:** footage-led scenes (stock or generated b-roll; free sources such as Pexels/Pixabay are fine) with text cards for stats, timelines, lists and quotes. Footage must not imply something false (e.g. no jet engines in a car-maker story).
7. **Review every card** for clipped text, overlaps and wrong numbers before upload.
8. **Cut-downs:** X cut under 140 s, LinkedIn 4:5, two 9:16 trailers, thumbnail.
9. **Schedule** per the calendar below, then write the Beehiiv case study draft and the LinkedIn/X/Facebook copy.
